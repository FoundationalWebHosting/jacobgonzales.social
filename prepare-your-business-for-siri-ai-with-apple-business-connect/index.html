Prepare Your Business for Siri AI with Apple Business Connect

Jacob Gonzales September 24, 2026

With the arrival of Siri AI, consumers are increasingly relying on intelligent assistants to automate daily tasks and quickly discover new information. To ensure your business gets noticed by these AI agents, it is essential to be indexed where they look first.

The most effective way to do this is through Apple Business Connect—a free resource that allows you to manage your company’s presence across the Apple ecosystem, including Maps, Wallet, Mail, and Siri AI. By utilizing this tool, you can customize your local listing with actionable features like reservation or ticketing buttons, ensuring customers see the most accurate and important information.

How to Get Started

  1. Visit the Website: Go to the Apple Business Connect website and click the blue “Get Started” button.
  2. Enter Business Details: Enter your organization’s legal name, business email, website, and address.
  3. Create an Account: Create a Managed Apple Account by providing your name, email, password, and phone number.
  4. Verify Account: Verify your account using the codes sent to your email and phone.
  5. Set Preferences: Agree to the terms and select the options that best fit your business size and goals.
  6. Manage Profiles: From the homepage, you can begin managing your Maps profile, Wallet profile, and employee IT tools.

Verification and Next Steps

Before diving in, verify your organization by clicking the banner at the top of the page. You will need to provide two verification documents, such as a business license or domain verification.

Once verified (typically within five business days), you will unlock a full suite of free branding services, including:

  • Location management
  • Branded Mail
  • Tap to Pay on iPhone
  • Brand Profiles

Apple Business Connect is a powerful, free tool to help you get discovered and connect with customers faster. Don’t get left behind as new AI technology rolls out—get started today!

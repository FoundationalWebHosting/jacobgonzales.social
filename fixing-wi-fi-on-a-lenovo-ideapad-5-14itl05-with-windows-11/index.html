Fixing Wi-Fi on a Lenovo IdeaPad 5 (14ITL05) with Windows 11

Jacob Gonzales January 19, 2023

Overview

When your Lenovo IdeaPad 5 (14ITL05) loses internet access—shown by the globe icon—you can fix the Wi-Fi driver using another computer.

Steps

  1. Prepare another device Use a separate laptop with internet to open Lenovo’s site. Search “Lenovo IdeaPad 5 (14ITL05) drivers” and download the newest Windows 11 Wi-Fi driver, or use the direct link.

  2. Transfer the driver Insert a USB drive into the working laptop and copy the downloaded file.

  3. Install on the IdeaPad Plug the USB into your Lenovo IdeaPad 5 (14ITL05), open the drive, and double-click 37sw0a0f43gwseb0.exe. Confirm administrator permission if prompted, then complete the installation.

  4. Restart Reboot the laptop so changes take effect.

Notes

  • Check Lenovo’s support site for the latest driver and other updates.
  • Back up essential files before updating.
